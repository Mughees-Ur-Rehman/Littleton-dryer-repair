How it works

Our Dryer Belt Replacement Process

We begin with a thorough inspection of your dryer to identify the problem and confirm the need for a belt replacement. This step typically takes about 30 minutes.

Our technicians carefully remove the damaged dryer belt using specialized tools like screwdrivers and wrenches, ensuring no additional parts are harmed during the process.

We install a high-quality replacement belt from trusted brands such as Whirlpool or LG. This step ensures your dryer operates smoothly and efficiently.

After installation, we conduct thorough testing to ensure the dryer functions correctly. We check for any unusual noises or issues during operation.

Finally, we walk you through the completed work, explaining what was done and providing maintenance tips to prolong the life of your new dryer belt.
